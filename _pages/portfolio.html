My research focuses on computational organometallic chemistry, particularly the construction and comparison of catalytic pathways. These project summaries describe my contributions without reporting unpublished structures or numerical results.

Ni-Catalysed Five-Membered Cyclisation

SURF 2025–26 · XJTLU

Transition-state searches for a five-membered cyclisation system, with particular attention to the coordination environment encountered during a difficult reductive-elimination search.

Ni/Zn Reductive Cross-Electrophile Coupling

SURF 2024–25 · XJTLU

A team investigation of coupling between heteroaryl bromides and tertiary alkyl halides, comparing oxidative-addition, single-electron-transfer and concerted proposals. My experience also included related cobalt-system calculations, constrained optimisations and computational-data processing.
