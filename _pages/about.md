## About

I am an undergraduate student at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, majoring in Chemistry with a focus on computational chemistry. My research uses density functional theory to study the mechanisms of transition-metal-catalysed reactions, particularly how nickel and zinc catalysts control selectivity in cross-coupling chemistry.

## Research

**Mechanistic study of nickel-catalysed annulation** *(Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship, 2025–26).*
Locating transition states for ring-closing pathways with Gaussian 16 at B3LYP/6-31G(d), using constrained-bond scans followed by frequency verification and intrinsic reaction coordinate calculations. The key conclusion is that the rate-determining step is a concerted C–C bond formation, reached only after adjusting the ring-precursor bond length and conformation parameters close to thirty times before convergence to the correct stationary point with a single imaginary frequency.

**Reductive cross-coupling of hetaryl bromides with tertiary alkyl halides** *(Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship, 2024–25, supervised by Prof. X).*
Three competing pathways were tested — oxidative addition, single-electron transfer, and a concerted mechanism — across toluene, THF, DMF and acetonitrile, using the SMD implicit solvation model. THF (ε = 7.43) gave the most consistent picture of the relative barriers. This project is also where the tool below came from.

## Tool

**Gaussian LogReader Pro** — a desktop program I designed for batch-extracting thermodynamic data from Gaussian `.log` files: SCF energy, thermal correction to Gibbs free energy, and total free energy, with relative-energy calculation, reaction-profile plotting, and PNG / PDF / SVG export. The interface is built with Tkinter; the code was written by Tencent Hy4preview. Available on [GitHub](https://github.com/Lightmessager/Gaussian09d-log-reader-Pro).
