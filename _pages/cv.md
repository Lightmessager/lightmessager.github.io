## Research Interests

I study the **free energy changes of organometallic compounds during catalytic processes** using **Density Functional Theory (DFT)** and **Gaussian**. My work covers construction and location of transition states and intermediates, isomerization where required, and mapping the most probable reaction landscape by comparing alternative pathways. I extend the scope to diverse substrates, solvent environments, and catalyst ligands to predict reaction outcomes and selectivity.

My broader interests lie in **materials and catalysis** — semiconductors, high-performance plastics, modified metal alloys — with potential extension to biopharmaceutical applications.

### 2025 – 2026 Summer · SURF Project with Dr. Xiaotai Wang

**Topic:** DFT study of a nickel-catalysed reductive coupling across two systems — a novel five-membered non-conjugated system and a seven-membered conjugated system derived from literature.

- Carried out the **majority of transition-state searches for the five-membered system**, making nearly **30 adjustments to bond lengths and conformations**.
- When convergence failed, collaborated with my supervisor to introduce a **carbonyl group** to facilitate reductive elimination.
- After one and a half months the final transition state remained non-convergent. I attributed this to the **lack of an exocyclic double bond**, which prevented the nickel complex from achieving a stable four-coordinate geometry — a conclusion reached through systematic diagnosis rather than abandonment.
- Authored **most of the final poster text**, managed all typesetting, and handled communication with the supervising professor.
