## Research Interests

---
I study the **free energy changes of organometallic compounds during catalytic processes** using **Density Functional Theory (DFT)** and **Gaussian**. My work covers construction and location of transition states and intermediates, isomerization where required, and mapping the most probable reaction landscape by comparing alternative pathways. The scope extends to diverse substrates, solvent environments, and catalyst ligands to predict reaction outcomes and selectivity.

My broader interests lie in **materials and catalysis** — semiconductors, high-performance plastics, modified metal alloys — with potential extension to biopharmaceutical applications.
