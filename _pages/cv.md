## Education

**Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU), Suzhou, China**  
BSc Applied Chemistry · September 2023 – June 2027 (expected)

Academic foundations in organic and inorganic chemistry, coordination chemistry and group theory, with an interest in connecting molecular structure, orbital descriptions and reaction mechanisms.

## Research Interests

Computational organometallic chemistry; density functional theory (DFT); catalytic reaction mechanisms; transition states and intermediates; catalyst design and selectivity.

My longer-term interests include using computation to understand and design functional materials, including semiconductors, high-performance polymers and metal-alloy catalysts.
