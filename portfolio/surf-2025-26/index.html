Ni-Catalysed Five-Membered Cyclisation

SURF 2025–26 · Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University
Supervisor: Dr Xiaotai Wang

Research Question

How can a plausible catalytic pathway be constructed for a nickel-catalysed five-membered cyclisation, and what can an unsuccessful transition-state search reveal about the proposed coordination environment?

Chemical System

The project examined a five-membered non-conjugated system alongside a literature-derived seven-membered conjugated system. This summary focuses on my work on the five-membered system.

Computational Approach

I used Gaussian for geometry optimisation, transition-state searches and energy processing. I explored alternative bond lengths and conformations to construct candidate geometries.

My Contribution

  • Carried out the majority of transition-state searches for the five-membered system.
  • Explored approximately 30 bond-length and conformational adjustments while examining how the nickel coordination environment affected the proposed pathway.
  • Proposed and discussed a CO-assisted alternative with my supervisor when the reductive-elimination search remained unsuccessful.
  • Wrote most of the final poster text, completed its typesetting and coordinated communication with the supervising professor.

Mechanistic Challenge

Repeated attempts to locate a reductive-elimination transition state did not converge. Inspection of the candidate structures suggested that an under-coordinated nickel centre might be contributing to the difficulty. This motivated consideration of additional CO coordination as an alternative mechanistic proposal.

The coordination argument is a working hypothesis. The unsuccessful search does not establish that the original pathway is impossible, and the proposed CO-assisted pathway is not presented here as a validated mechanism.

Results and Validation

My main contribution was the transition-state search and subsequent mechanistic diagnosis. No claim of a fully validated cyclisation or reductive-elimination pathway is made in this summary. A final assignment requires the relevant stationary-point, vibrational-mode and connectivity evidence to be considered together.

Output
