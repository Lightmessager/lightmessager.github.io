Computational Study of a Metal-Catalysed Skeletal-Editing Reaction

SURF 2025–26 · Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University
Supervisor: Dr Xiaotai Wang

Research Question

How transferable is a literature-reported mechanism across related lactone systems, and what can a difficult reductive-elimination transition-state search reveal about the proposed coordination environment?

Chemical System

The literature reference involved a benzene-fused six-membered conjugated lactone. Our project examined related systems, including a five-membered non-conjugated lactone and a seven-membered conjugated lactone. My work focused mainly on the five-membered system and on testing whether the reported mechanistic picture could be carried over to that substrate class.

Computational Approach

I used Gaussian for geometry optimisation, transition-state searches and energy processing. For the difficult reductive-elimination step, I systematically varied starting geometries, key bond distances, conformations and coordination models to construct and test alternative transition-state guesses.

My Contribution

  • Carried out the majority of transition-state searches for the five-membered non-conjugated system.
  • Conducted more than 30 systematic transition-state search attempts by varying initial geometries, bond distances, conformations and coordination models.
  • Examined the nickel coordination environment and discussed a CO-assisted reductive-elimination alternative with my supervisor when the original search strategy remained unsuccessful.
  • Prepared most of the final poster content, layout and typesetting, and coordinated communication with the supervising professor.

Mechanistic Challenge

Repeated attempts to locate the reductive-elimination transition state for the five-membered system did not converge to the desired stationary point. Comparing these attempts with the related systems suggested that the literature mechanism was not straightforwardly transferable to the five-membered non-conjugated substrate.

Inspection of the candidate structures also suggested that an under-coordinated nickel centre might be contributing to the difficulty. This motivated consideration of additional CO coordination as a possible alternative. The coordination argument remains a working hypothesis: the unsuccessful search does not establish that the original pathway is impossible, and the proposed CO-assisted route is not presented here as a validated mechanism.

Results and Validation

My main contribution was the transition-state search and subsequent mechanistic diagnosis. No claim of a fully validated reductive-elimination pathway is made in this summary. A final mechanistic assignment would require the relevant stationary-point, vibrational-mode and connectivity evidence to be considered together.

Output

I contributed to the project poster. See Publications & Presentations for the available poster file.
