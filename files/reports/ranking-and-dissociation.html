04 / INTERPRETATION BOUNDARIES

Keep the comparison honest.

What was controlled

New 6.2 runs use fresh conversations, matching prompts and byte-identical copies of the 17 anonymous XYZ files. Only geometric descriptors are permitted. No quantum chemistry, force-field evaluation, optimization or fabricated energies.

Repeats are not retries after feedback. No answer key or other run’s result is intentionally provided.

Separated directories and prompt restrictions are not OS-level isolation. Sharing a machine or project leaves potential access paths. A clean observed tool trace cannot prove universal non-exposure.

What was not controlled

This is the same previously used question. Fresh conversations do not make it a new unseen benchmark, or establish absence of prior exposure or training contamination. Small-sample variation must not be read as a general capability verdict.

Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 were labeled High by the owner; their final answers, rather than fully audited execution traces, were supplied. Directory-name exposure was reported, and Fable explicitly acknowledged departure from input-only access. This does not establish that either read the answer key.

The older Astra result has no securely established version. High, xhigh and Max are not matched compute budgets.

Reference provenance and scope

The energy comparison reuses historical evidence, not a new calculation. Raw output support was located for Q, K, N, E, O, H, D and G; values for B, C, L, A and F are report-derived because their original outputs were not relocated. Do not mix these ORCA relative energies with Gaussian absolute energies, or interpret single-point ranking as experimental stability or free energy.

Observed timings may reflect concurrent scheduling and tool overhead. No calibrated energy consumption, token-cost or performance-per-dollar conclusion is supported. Scores are descriptive: the 78 comparisons share structures and are not 78 independent experimental trials.

Two clocks, two meanings

347.55 seconds is the 24-core ORCA single-point computation time. It is not the model's answering time.

13 min 12 sec spans the model's first read to its final proposed export. It is not pure reasoning time; the native approval wait is not counted as thought time.

The model generated the scientific candidate. After a native write-approval wait, the coordinator materialized the exact final script through the approved executor without changing its scientific content.

What this result does not prove

No geometry optimization or frequency calculation was performed on this new candidate for the score. The result does not establish a minimum, kinetic preference, dissociation mechanism, barrier or experimental product distribution.

The original task targeted Gaussian M06L with solvent. The standardized check uses ORCA 6.1.1 B97-3c TightSCF, gas phase, charge 0 and multiplicity 1. A lower score here does not establish improved performance under the original target method.

All nine historical output records were checked for matching hashes and normal completion. This is one new model trial, not a repeated Test2 estimate. Browser visual QA remains unperformed; the report is validated programmatically.
